IN THE APPELLATE COURT OF CIVIL APPEALS

Case No. 2026-CV-4482
Appeal from the Superior Court, Commercial Division

OPINION AND ORDER

Per Curiam:

This appeal arises from an action instituted by the plaintiff seeking specific performance and monetary damages for breach of a commercial covenant. Following a jury trial, the trial court granted the defendant's motion to dismiss the suit on the grounds of insufficient consideration.

The plaintiff timely filed an appeal alleging procedural error by the presiding judge in entertaining the post-verdict motion. The appellant contends that adequate consideration was present in the form of mutual promises exchanged upon execution of the underlying instrument.

Upon review of the trial record, this court determines that the trial judge acted within judicial discretion. The motion presented questions of law regarding contractual enforceability. Consequently, the judgment of the Superior Court is affirmed, and no further relief is granted.
